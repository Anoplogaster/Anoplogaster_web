        ## Optional Analytics

        This website uses Google Analytics to help me understand how people discover and use my scientific website. Analytics stays off until you choose **Allow analytics**. Declining does not affect the website, Photography, or Angry Anglerfish. You can change your choice through **Privacy preferences** in the footer.

        After you allow it, Google's tag may collect browser/device and usage information, such as page visits and interactions, and set first-party Analytics cookies. This site's configuration disables Google signals and advertising personalization. Page addresses sent by this configuration exclude query strings and fragments, and referrers are limited to their origin. Google's account-controlled measurement and retention settings are separate from these website controls.

        Your preference is saved in this browser. Analytics cookies created by this configuration have a 30-day lifetime. Withdrawing permission disables this site's Analytics sending and normally reloads the current page to unload the tag; it does not delete information Google has already received. If the browser cannot replace an earlier saved permission, a warning explains that other pages may still use it. Browser blocking or unavailable storage can affect Analytics and whether your choice is remembered. With JavaScript disabled, optional Analytics does not load.

        Read [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for information about Google's processing. This explanation describes the website's implementation; it is not a legal-compliance certification.

        ## Angry Anglerfish and local storage

        Angry Anglerfish saves your personal best, discoveries, Learning Mode choice, and sound preferences in your browser. Its own code does not send those records to a server. There is no public leaderboard and no game account is required. The Analytics choice does not erase saved game data. Clearing this site's browser storage removes saved preferences and game progress.

        ## Website delivery and contact

        Pages and images are delivered by Netlify. The Analytics choice controls optional Google Analytics, not the ordinary requests needed to load the website. Hosting/account settings and provider retention are separate from the browser controls described here.
